VISUAL PUNS

The Scroll of the Ancient Miracles was discovered during excavations of shards and fragments that were found
in the Qumran Caves overlooking the Dead Sea. The archeologists were able to put together some of shards and
fragments. The emissary of His Holiness the Pope and the Chief Rabbi of Jerusalem could decipher some passages
in the original Aramaic and ancient Greek. They found out that these were called the Book of Ancient Miracles.

The first miracle discovered was:

"On the day of the Sabbath, the Lord spoketh among the disciples of Jesus, and commanded, Beholdeth thou the miracle
of the noseless fisherman, whosoever caught a herring and smelt."
